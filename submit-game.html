Submit Your Indie Game

Want your indie game featured on PixelFair? Send us your game details and our team can review your request.

Requests go to: services@digitalinfohub.net

Alternative contact: artifyspace@icloud.com (secondary — the primary recipient remains services@digitalinfohub.net)

How this form works: PixelFair is a static website with no backend. When you complete the form and press Request Game Submission, your browser validates the fields and then opens your own email application with everything prefilled. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted in the background, or stored by this website, and the request is only sent once you press Send in your mail app.

Game submission request form

A few sentences is plenty — you can always email more detail later.

You must tick the consent checkbox before requesting a submission.

No email app opened? That means your device has no default mail client configured — the form itself cannot send anything. Fallback options:
• Use this direct link: mailto:services@digitalinfohub.net
• Or copy your details and email services@digitalinfohub.net manually from any webmail service.
• Secondary contact: artifyspace@icloud.com.

Submission Status

  • ✅ Email-based listing requests — available now (this form).
  • Coming Soon Automated web submissions (no email app needed).
  • Coming Soon Public game listings beyond current examples.
  • Coming Soon Review automation & submission status dashboard.

Please note: an emailed request is a request for review — it does not automatically publish a game listing. Our team reads every request, but publication depends on manual review.
