About PixelFair

Every Indie Game Deserves to Be Seen.

Our Mission

PixelFair exists to help independent game developers gain visibility. Great indie games often disappear under the noise of major storefront algorithms — not because they lack quality, but because they lack exposure. PixelFair aims to be a dedicated discovery shelf for those games: a place where players come specifically to find something new, strange, cosy, challenging or wonderful that a small team made.

We are currently in an early, static launch phase. Many platform features — automated submissions, verified developer profiles, review tooling and public listings beyond our examples — are honestly labelled Coming Soon rather than pretended into existence.

What PixelFair Is For

For Developers

Showcase your game with a proper detail page, artwork, screenshots and links you control. Request a listing today by email; self-service publishing is Coming Soon.

For Players

Browse and search indie games by genre — Action, RPG, Horror, Simulation and more — and discover titles you would probably never have found otherwise.

For the Community

Dev logs, tutorials, spotlights and resources on the blog, plus clear community and developer guidelines so the platform stays respectful and fair.

Intended Audience

  • Independent developers and small studios who want a free, straightforward way to be noticed.
  • Players seeking discovery — people who actively enjoy finding hidden gems across genres like Platformer, Puzzle, Strategy, Racing and Multiplayer.
  • Curators and press looking for an organised index of upcoming indie projects (listing export tooling: Coming Soon).

We deliberately do not publish invented audience statistics, user counts or partnership claims. When we can report real numbers, we will.

Planned Submission Process

  1. Today: developers complete the Submit a Game form, which opens their own email app with a prefilled request to services@digitalinfohub.net.
  2. Manual review: the team reads each emailed request. A request is an invitation to review — not an automatic publication.
  3. Listing created: if approved, the game receives a detail page marked “Coming Soon” until official release information is provided by the developer.
  4. Future (Coming Soon): direct web-based submissions, status dashboards, developer accounts and automated moderation.

How We Operate

  • Honesty first: unlaunched features say “Coming Soon”; forms never claim data was sent when it was not.
  • No fabrication: no fake reviews, ratings, dates, authors or partnerships.
  • Privacy by design: the site is static; contact forms use your own email client via mailto: and store nothing on this website.
  • Accessibility & performance: keyboard-friendly, reduced-motion aware, responsive from small phones to large desktops.
