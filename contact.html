Get Featured & Get in Touch

Want to feature your game on PixelFair? Contact services@digitalinfohub.net to request a listing. Send us your game details and we will review your request.

📧 Primary contact: services@digitalinfohub.net

Alternative (secondary): artifyspace@icloud.com

How this form works: PixelFair has no backend. Completing the form and pressing Send Contact Message validates your input, then opens your own email application with a prefilled message to services@digitalinfohub.net. Nothing is uploaded or stored by this site, and the message is only sent when you press Send.

General contact form

Tip: for full game-detail forms use the dedicated Submit a Game page.

No email app configured? The form cannot send anything on its own. Please:
• Open your webmail and write to services@digitalinfohub.net; or
• Use this direct link: mailto:services@digitalinfohub.net; or
• Email the secondary address artifyspace@icloud.com.
