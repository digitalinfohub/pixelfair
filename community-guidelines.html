Community Guidelines

How we keep PixelFair fair, honest and welcoming for indie developers and players alike.

Last updated: October 2026 · Contact: services@digitalinfohub.net

1. What these guidelines cover

These Community Guidelines apply to everyone who uses PixelFair: visitors browsing listings, developers submitting games, commenters (comments are Coming Soon) and anyone corresponding with the team by email.

2. Respectful conduct

  • Treat developers, players and staff with respect behind every project there is a person.
  • Critique games, not people. No harassment, threats, slurs or targeted abuse.
  • No discrimination or hateful content based on protected characteristics under the UK Equality Act 2010.
  • Keep discussions about games. Off-site drama and brigading are not welcome.

3. Honest submissions & listings

  • Only submit games you have the right to promote, and only with accurate information.
  • Do not impersonate another developer, studio or game.
  • Spam, duplicate submissions and fake engagement (votes, reviews once they exist) may be removed.
  • Listings marked “Coming Soon” reflect genuine pre-release status — never treat them as available products.

4. Prohibited content

We do not accept content that is: illegal; infringes copyright or trademarks; contains malware or phishing links; depicts sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance — reported to authorities); promotes real-world violence; or is aimed at doxxing or swatting others.

5. Moderation approach

Moderation is currently manual and email-based (services@digitalinfohub.net). Automated moderation tooling is Coming Soon. We aim for proportionate responses: removal of offending content first, temporary exclusion only where needed, and always an explanation when we act.

6. Reporting problems

Email reports with a link/screenshot, what rule was broken and why it matters. We acknowledge reports when resources allow and never guarantee instant action — but copyright takedown requests are handled per the Copyright Policy.

7. Changes to these guidelines

We may update this page; the “last updated” date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced on the PixelFair Updates blog category when it launches (Coming Soon).
